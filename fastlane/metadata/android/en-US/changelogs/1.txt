Initial release of babalon JS for Android:
• 3D scene composer and model viewer (GLB, glTF, OBJ, STL, FBX, PLY, USDZ).
• Touch controls, lighting presets, HDR environments, and materials editor.
• Character posing, automatic rigging, and animation playback.
• Surface measurements and smart alignment guides.
• Photo mode with cinematic post-processing filters.
• Augmented Reality (ARCore) with real-world surface placement.
• 100% offline and open-source.
